About

A conference for college students

WILDE-Student Conference is for college students. It is held at G.B. Pant Memorial Govt. College, Rampur — Rampur College — in Rampur Bushahr, Himachal Pradesh.

The theme is interdisciplinary tools for students. Over two days, the programme is talks and workshops. Any student may come to the talks, with or without registering. The workshops are only for registered participants.

Registration is limited to 200 students, because the workshops cannot take a large group. The fee is ₹150, a small amount so that students who register intend to attend.

  1. Research and internships

    Build skills for research and for industry-based internships.

  2. Interdisciplinary skills

    Work across subjects on questions that do not sit in one syllabus.

  3. Rational thinking

    Encourage an inquisitive nature, and the habit of thinking carefully.
